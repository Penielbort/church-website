A Message from the Priest In Charge

Rev. Fr. Emmanuel Amarh

Priest-in-Charge & Chairman PCC · St. Peter Anglican Church, Nungua

Welcome to St. Peter Anglican Church, Nungua — a community of faith rooted in the rich traditions of the Anglican Communion. Founded on 29th June 1940 by Godfried Borketey Bortey and his brothers, our church has been a beacon of hope and spiritual growth for the people of Nungua and beyond for over 80 years.

We are a diverse congregation united in our love for Christ and our commitment to serving our community. Whether you are a lifelong Anglican, a seeker of faith, or simply looking for a spiritual home — you will find here a welcome as warm as the Ghanaian sun. Come, let us worship together.

Our Heritage · Est. 29th June 1940

About St. Peter Anglican Church

Founded on 29th June 1940 — a beacon of faith, education and community in Nungua for over 80 years.

Our Story

A Legacy of Faith Since 1940

In the late 1930s, as Nungua's population grew, there was a need for another church beyond the existing Presbyterian Church. The vision for St Peter Anglican Church was conceived by the late Godfried Borketey Bortey (Denfutse), who invited his brothers Lawrence Borketey Borquaye and Ebenezer Bortier Borquaye to join him in this sacred mission.

After consultations with Rev. Fr. A. K. Nelson (later Assistant Bishop) and the Rt. Rev. Dr. John Orfeur Aglionby, then Bishop of Accra, our church was born. St Peter's Anglican Church was inaugurated on 29th June 1940 at 3:30 pm, coinciding with the Feast of the Apostles Peter and Paul.

The first place of worship was the ground floor of a building belonging to Mr. L. B. Borquaye's father. As the congregation grew, a school was established in 1950, and the present site was acquired. The current church building was completed in 1980 — the cornerstone plaque unveiled by Mr. John Anangfio Tetteh — and consecrated on 4th February 1984 by Rt. Rev. F. W. B. Thompson. The church attained Parochial Status in July 1983.

Today, St Peter's continues to be a beacon of hope and a centre for spiritual growth, with out-stations at St. Augustine's (started 1992) and St. Monica Baatsonaa (started 2003), and the St Peter the Rock Senior High School officially started in 2003.

We are a diverse congregation united in our love for Christ and our commitment to serving our community. Whether you're seeking spiritual growth, community connection, or a place to worship — everyone is welcome at St Peter's.

Honouring Our Founders

Our Founding Fathers

The visionaries who, in faith and sacrifice, established St Peter's Anglican Church in 1940.

Godfried Borketey Bortey
Principal Founder · Denfutse
The visionary who conceived the idea of establishing St Peter's Anglican Church in Nungua. His leadership and dedication laid the foundation for what would become a spiritual home for generations.
Lawrence Borketey Borquaye
Co-Founder
Brother to Godfried, he provided the ground floor of his father's building as the first place of worship. His generosity and commitment were instrumental in the church's early establishment.
Ebenezer Bortier Borquaye
Co-Founder
The third brother who joined the sacred mission. His partnership with his brothers ensured the successful founding and growth of St Peter's Anglican Church.

Means of Grace

The Holy Sacraments

💧
Holy Baptism

In Baptism, we are made members of the Body of Christ and given new birth by water and the Holy Spirit. All are welcome — infants and adults.

🕊
Confirmation

Public affirmation of baptismal vows, administered by the Bishop with prayer and the laying on of hands. Classes held annually during Lent.

🍞
Holy Eucharist

The central act of Anglican worship — the breaking of bread in remembrance of Christ's once-for-all sacrifice on the Cross. Every Sunday 7:00 AM.

💍
Holy Matrimony

The sacred covenant of marriage made before God. Couples must meet with the Vicar at least 3 months in advance to arrange preparation classes.

🙌
Holy Orders

The setting apart of Deacons, Priests, and Bishops through solemn prayer and the laying on of hands in the apostolic succession.

🫒
Anointing of the Sick

Healing prayer with the anointing of holy oil for the sick and suffering, following the apostolic practice in the Epistle of James.
